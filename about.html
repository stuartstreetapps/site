About the studio

Small apps, built with care.

Stuart Street Apps is an independent iOS studio based in Arlington, Virginia.

The studio builds focused learning apps, starting with Learn About Your United States.

Good software should be useful, approachable, and respectful of your time. That means no app ads, no app tracking, and no dark patterns.

Current Focus

Today, the focus is helping people learn all 50 states through capitals, facts, the U.S. map, state stats, and quizzes in a clean, rewarding iPhone and iPad experience. As more apps are developed, this site will grow around the same simple idea: useful tools that feel good to use.

Learn About Your United States

Explore capitals, facts, the U.S. map, size and population details, quizzes, and medals as you learn all 50 states.
