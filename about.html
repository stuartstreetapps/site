About the studio

Small apps, built with care.

Stuart Street Apps is an independent iOS studio based in Arlington, Virginia.

The studio builds focused apps for curious people, starting with Learn About Your United States.

Good software should be useful, approachable, and respectful of your time. That means no app ads, no app tracking, and no dark patterns.

Current Focus

Today, the focus is helping people learn U.S. geography through a clean, rewarding iPhone and iPad experience. As more apps are developed, this site will grow around the same simple idea: useful tools that feel good to use.

Learn About Your United States

Explore maps, practice capitals, identify flags, and earn medals as you master all 50 states.
