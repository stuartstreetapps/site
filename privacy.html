Privacy Policy

Applies to: Learn About Your United States  ·  Last updated: June 2025

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how Stuart Street Apps handles information in connection with Learn About Your United States.

🔒

Short version: Learn About Your United States does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No information is transmitted off your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require you to create an account, and no usage data, analytics, or identifiers are gathered or sent anywhere.

Data Storage

All app data — including your quiz progress and earned medals — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard local storage. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking frameworks.

Children's Privacy

Because we collect no data at all, Learn About Your United States is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
