Support

Help and information for all Stuart Street Apps.

✅ Getting started

  • Download free from the App Store
  • Tap any state on the map to begin exploring
  • Choose a quiz mode from the home screen
  • Earn medals by improving your score

🏅 Medals & progress

  • Bronze, Silver, Gold — one set per quiz type
  • Progress saves automatically on your device
  • Complete all 50 states to earn Gold
  • Tap any medal to view its requirements

❓ Quiz modes

  • Capitals, flags, and geography
  • Quick Practice — 10 questions
  • Challenge Mode — 30 questions
  • Complete Challenge — all 50 states
